Scaling Through Phases

The Gradial engine "warms up" over each phase — Phase 1 starts with 1 template and ~100 pages, Phase 2 scales to 2 templates and 1,000 pages, and Phase 3 can handle 6+ templates and 20,000+ pages concurrently. Parallel processing enables exponentially more throughput in each subsequent phase.
